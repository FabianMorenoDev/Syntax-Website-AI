## Syntax Monogram

### `syntax_monogramm_dark_RGB.png`

* **Usage:**
  For light backgrounds (e.g. white website sections, footer with white background).
* **Placement:**

  * Header/Navigation (top left)
  * Meta tags & social sharing
  * Website footer (light version)
* **Note:**
  Always leave sufficient white space. Do not use at a height smaller than **32px**.

---

### `syntax_monogramm_white.png`

* **Usage:**
  For dark backgrounds (e.g. Deep Purple `#060f38` or black).
* **Placement:**

  * Footer (on dark background)
  * Overlay on images/videos
  * Hero sections
* **Note:**
  Always ensure sufficient contrast.

## AZAV Certification Seal

### `azav-logo-navy.png`

* **Usage:**
  For light backgrounds. Indicates AZAV certification of the educational offering.
* **Placement:**

  * Below course descriptions
  * Footer (in formal contexts)
  * Trust sections / Quality assurance notes
* **Note:**
  Ideally used with a note like:
  *“Our courses are AZAV-certified and eligible for funding through education vouchers.”*

---

### `azav-logo-white.png`

* **Usage:**
  For dark sections or dark mode.
* **Placement:**

  * Trust banner or footer on dark background
  * Slides, info pages, hero sections
* **Note:**
  Ensure contrast; optionally place on a light background surface.

## Federal Employment Agency Logo

### `Bundesagentur_für_Arbeit_logo.svg`

* **Usage:**
  Only in the context of government funding, such as:

  * Info box about education vouchers
  * Funding notes on landing pages
  * Trust or partner section
* **Note:**
  Use only in non-promotional contexts with an explanatory note. Never distort or recolor. Always keep the original version.
