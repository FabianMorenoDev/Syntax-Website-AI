# Team Portrait Usage Guidelines

These images represent official team portraits of the **Syntax team**, including the **CEO** and **Sales representatives**. They are used to build trust and encourage engagement — especially in areas like **consultation booking**, **about pages**, and **team introductions**.

---

## General Usage Principles

* **Purpose**: Humanize the brand, highlight expertise, and support trust-driven CTAs like “Book a call”.
* **Style**: Professional yet approachable; some team members wear Syntax-branded apparel.

---

## Layout Recommendations

* **Consultation CTA Blocks**:
  Use alongside buttons like “Talk to our team” or “Schedule a free consultation”.

* **Team Grid**:
  Group sales staff into a uniform grid with name and role underneath. Recommended size: `200x200px`, rounded corners, or in a carousel

* **Responsive Design**:
  Use 1–2 per row on mobile, 3–4 on desktop.

---

## Do Not

* Use images without names or roles attached.
* Apply heavy filters or replace backgrounds.
* Use portraits in casual/meme contexts.
