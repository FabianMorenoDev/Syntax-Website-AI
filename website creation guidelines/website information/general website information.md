## Title  
**Syntax Institut: Weiterbildungen mit Bildungsgutschein**

---

## Meta Information  
Das Syntax Institut ist einer der führenden Anbieter von geförderten Weiterbildungen für Arbeitssuchende im Bereich IT & Entwicklung. ➤ Lass dich beraten

---

## Type of Website  
**Conversion-optimierte Marketing-Website** für eine zertifizierte Bildungseinrichtung im Tech-Bereich. Fokus: IT & Entwicklung, gefördert über Bildungsgutschein (AZAV).

---

## Goal of Website  
Maximise the number of **qualified consultation bookings** with the sales team.  
Secondary goals:
- Build **trust** and brand credibility  
- Present the products (course options)  
- **Explain** how the Bildungsgutschein works  
- Highlight **course benefits** and success outcomes  
- Generate **email and phone leads**

---

## Target Audience  
- Job seekers in Germany (with or without tech background)  
- People eligible for a **Bildungsgutschein**  
- Career changers looking to enter **IT & Development**  
- Motivated, often non-technical individuals (age 25–55)

---

## Visual / UI Design Requirements  
- Use the official **Syntax color palette**  
- Typography:  
  - **Headlines**: Clash Display  
  - **Body Text**: Clash Grotesk  
- UI Elements:  
  - Rounded corners  
  - Soft shadows  
  - Modular card-based layout  
- Color Mood:  
  - Light/pastel backgrounds  
  - Bold accent colors (Yellow, Deep Purple)  
- Imagery:  
  - Use **real team members** and **student portraits** over stock photos  
- Layout:  
  - **Mobile-first**, fast loading, large buttons, clear CTAs

---

## Funnel Strategy  
The website should **direct users into the consultation funnel**

This funnel is the primary conversion goal — the site should guide users toward this booking journey clearly and quickly (via CTAs like "Jetzt beraten lassen").
