# Stock Image Usage Guidelines

These images represent collaborative, inclusive, and modern work environments. They should be used to visually support the tone and values of Syntax across marketing, onboarding, and digital platforms.

---

### `co-working-people-working-together.jpg`

* **Description**: Three young adults (diverse group) smiling and looking at a phone together in a casual indoor setting.
* **Tone**: Friendly, digital-native, community-driven.
* **Use for**:

  * Landing pages promoting tech courses or app development
  * Community or student success sections
  * Testimonials or reviews
* **Don’ts**:

  * Avoid using in overly formal business contexts
  * Do not crop out individuals

---

### `colleagues-discussing-new-ideas-business-meeting.jpg`

* **Description**: Group of professionals brainstorming at a table with documents, markers, and a whiteboard.
* **Tone**: Creative, productive, focused teamwork.
* **Use for**:

  * Project-based learning modules
  * Product development & UX/UI design topics
  * Course modules related to agile, design thinking, or prototyping
* **Don’ts**:

  * Avoid using it in social or casual contexts

---

### ☕ `menschen-die-spass-ihrer-pause-haben.jpg`

* **Description**: Group of coworkers laughing and chatting around a table with coffee and devices.
* **Tone**: Relaxed, social, modern workplace culture.
* **Use for**:

  * Campus or team culture pages
  * Breaks & informal community moments
  * Social media posts or Slack/Discord integrations
* **Don’ts**:

  * Avoid using as the main image for serious, instructional content
